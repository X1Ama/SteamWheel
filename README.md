Steam Wheel

Can't decide what to play? Spin the wheel.

Steam Wheel is a small Windows app that ends the "what should I play?" problem. It finds the Steam account signed in on your PC, builds a list of your games, and puts them on a wheel. Press SPIN and it picks one.

Every game shows your achievement progress in Steam's style: how many you've unlocked, a progress bar, and how many are left. The winner's card shows the same progress, so you can tell at a glance whether it's a fresh start or nearly finished.

FEATURES
- Spin to pick: a smooth spinning wheel with a Steam-style look, and a history of recent winners.
- Your library, automatically: it detects the signed-in Steam account and builds your list from the games Steam already knows on your PC.
- Achievements under each game: unlocked count, progress bar and how many are left.
- Full control: switch any game off, search your library, and take each winner off the wheel after a spin if you like.
- One-row filters: Favorites (your Steam Favorites collection), Free (free-to-play games) and Family (games shared through Steam Family).
- Delisted games included: games Steam no longer sells still show up.
- Optional Steam key: adds your complete account, including games you never installed.
- Smooth and sharp: crisp text, smooth scrolling and a layout that scales with your display.
- Easy to update: it offers new versions with a clean update window.
- Private: everything stays on your PC. Nothing is uploaded, and your list is stored locally.

REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10 or 11 with Steam installed and signed in at least once. No installer needed, because it's a single exe.
